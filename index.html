[ about ]

analogue ghost is an experimental sound artist exploring the tension between human connection, machine logic, and the natural world.

using hardware synthesisers, field recordings, and generative processes, the work asks uncomfortable questions without offering clean answers.

based in Medellín, Colombia
available for live performance, collaboration, and installation.
